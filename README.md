# ElectraGuard

### AI-Powered Crowdsourced Election Monitoring & Incident Intelligence

ElectraGuard is a civic intelligence platform designed to transform scattered citizen reports into structured, prioritized election-monitoring intelligence.

## Problem

During elections, incidents such as voter intimidation, polling-station disruption, violence, accessibility issues, and equipment failures can be difficult to identify and prioritize at scale.

Traditional monitoring relies heavily on manual observation and verification, creating information bottlenecks.

## Solution

ElectraGuard provides:

- Multi-channel citizen incident reporting
- AI-assisted incident classification and triage
- Severity-based prioritization
- Trust-aware crowdsourcing
- Evidence and corroboration tracking
- Real-time incident mapping
- Observer verification workflows
- Alerts and operational analytics

## Core Principle

AI performs triage and information structuring — it does not determine whether an incident is true.

Verification is based on evidence, corroboration, and human review.
